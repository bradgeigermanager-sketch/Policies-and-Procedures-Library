### 3. Procedural Integration Notes
 * **The "Regret" Protocol:** Within your system, the Formal Statement of Regret should be categorized as an OFFICIAL_RECORD_OF_ADHERENCE_FAILURE. This ensures that even when an exception is necessary, the systemic preference for adherence is clearly documented.
 * **Closed-Loop Reconciliation:** Link this PEA output to your existing Sovereign Information System (SIS). If the Proposed Policy Revision receives a High priority, the system should automatically generate a Task_Item for your library maintenance protocol to update the base policy document.
 * **Version Control:** Ensure that when a policy is updated based on an Improvement Request from this form, the Policy ID is iterated (e.g., CORP-SEC-001 -> CORP-SEC-001.1), maintaining a clear lineage of how "unusual circumstances" drove organizational maturity.
